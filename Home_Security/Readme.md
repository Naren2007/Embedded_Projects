Home Security System :

In this project, I have used the ESP32-CAM module, PIR Sensor and integrated it with a Telegram bot which I have created for this purpose.

So, basically, what does this project do?
         Let's say someone is crossing your street, the PIR sensor will detect the motion and give the signal to the ESP32-CAM. The ESP32-CAM takes a photo of the moving 
         thing and it will send that photo to my Telegram account through the telegram BOT which i have created. I have made this possible with chatID and BOTID

What is the outer covering?
         To make the outer covering structure, I simply took a plastic box that was available in my home, and I made a hole for the CAMERA and SENSOR.
         
Challenges I faced :
        HEAT. The only challenge i faced with the system is the heavy heat dissipation such that the plastic melts. so how did I solve this problem?
        I made multiple small holes to ensure proper air circulation. This helped me to overcome the heavy heat dissipation.

Is it reliable?
        Absolutely YES..! It has been months since I have actually turned up the system, and till now it's working perfectly without any problem and still its sending 
        thousands of photos to me every single day. Guess what? Even if you are on another continent, it can still send photos to you!!
        Rain isn't a big problem for me because I chose a place where it is covered with a metal sheet
